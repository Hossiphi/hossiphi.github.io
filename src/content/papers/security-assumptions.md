Every security argument rests on assumptions — about the environment, about
other components, about how a system is used. When these assumptions stay
implicit, nobody notices when they stop holding. This poster paper proposes a
theory of security-related assumptions as a basis for making them explicit and
managing them throughout the software life cycle.
