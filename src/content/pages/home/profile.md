I am a computer scientist (M.Sc.) from Karlsruhe, Germany, and a researcher at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) since 2020.

My research lies at the intersection of software engineering, security, and requirements engineering.
I work on making security knowledge explicit throughout the software life cycle: security requirements, the assumptions a system's security rests on, and the trace links that connect them to architecture and code.
Currently, I focus on secure software engineering for energy systems within the [KASTEL Security Research Labs](https://kastel-labs.de/), for example with the EVerest dataset for secure software engineering and a theory of security-related assumptions.
Before that, I worked on traceability link recovery in [ARDoCo](https://ardoco.de).

I also teach at KIT, for example the lecture Software Engineering II, seminars on software architecture and security, and practical courses. You can find more on [the website of the research group I am working for](https://mcse.kastel.kit.edu/staff_sophie_corallo.php).
