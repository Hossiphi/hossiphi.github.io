Curriculum Vitae

A researcher at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology since 2020. In my research, I make security knowledge explicit throughout the software life cycle: security requirements, the assumptions a system's security rests on, and the trace links that connect them to architecture and code. Currently, I apply this to energy systems within the KASTEL Security Research Labs.
