Sophie Corallo

Computer scientist and researcher in secure software engineering.

I am a computer scientist (M.Sc.) from Karlsruhe, Germany, and a researcher at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) since 2020.

My research lies at the intersection of software engineering, security, and requirements engineering. I work on making security knowledge explicit throughout the software life cycle: security requirements, the assumptions a system’s security rests on, and the trace links that connect them to architecture and code. Currently, I focus on secure software engineering for energy systems within the KASTEL Security Research Labs, for example with the EVerest dataset for secure software engineering and a theory of security-related assumptions. Before that, I worked on traceability link recovery in ARDoCo.

I also teach at KIT, for example the lecture Software Engineering II, seminars on software architecture and security, and practical courses. You can find more on the website of the research group I am working for.
